hey, i'm Han

current work as product in dataiku. previously, did a phd in humn-computer interaction in inria, where i built with the team several cool UI prototypes. i decided to go to product to learn how to turn these prototypes into actual products.

in spring 2023, with two friends, we built a local speech-to-text transcription tool called lignes. initially built for help my girlfriend mum to transcribe her psychology meetings with patients. it is now used by sociologist, social science students and documentary makers. Many free alternatives appeared later, so we stopped.

currently, i am building a chrome extension that help me to do my monthly internet and phone refund. many colleagues seem to like it a lot. i learnt a lot from building both of these small products

i learnt a lot from building both of these small products. it makes me realize that i enjoy a lot in putting products into people's hands and see their reactions and how they use it. it becomes clear to me that most of what i do is all centered around people, exchanging with people, creating things for people and writing stories about people.

most of time i live through a day without knowing what happened today and what worth remembering. so i decided to write stories about people i meet that i find interesting.

i teach sometimes in the university about HCI, product and career. i write down my teaching in this doc.

I am always happy to chat if you want to ping me at hanhan.evan@gmail.com
